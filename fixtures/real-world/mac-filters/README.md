foreground. Open Genre, collect one bounded state and native PNG, Escape; open

## Limits

Canonical product path is verified; its exact source commit is not.
Exact per-element AX bounds and hit regions were unavailable in the chosen tool.
AX and pixels are separately acquired evidence, not atomic capture or a timing test.
Narrow fallback, result lookup, selection application, Clear, outside-click and
background hit-testing are not proven by these samples.
