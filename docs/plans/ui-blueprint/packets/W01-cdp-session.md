## Required state behavior

- Keep one transport IO owner and original absolute deadline/limits per operation;
  never re-enqueue a possibly sent command to recover backpressure. Preserve typed
  transport send uncertainty. No automatic retry/reconnect/target discovery.
- Match the exact active request ID and its bound CDP session/context. A reply from
  another session, a duplicate, expired/cancelled/unknown ID or old connection epoch
  cannot satisfy a new ticket. ID overflow refuses, never wraps/reuses. Use a
  defensible exact wire integer range, not lossy numeric coercion.
- Define finite caller-provided pending/result/event/message budgets and their
  actual ownership; reserve before sending/retaining. No unlimited table/queue or
  payload copy hidden behind a byte counter. Total allocations still belong to
  the future guarded worker; this slice cannot claim D05 enforcement by itself.
- Decode only necessary envelopes with pinned serde; retain payload as bounded
  data for its owning method decoder. Reject malformed/ambiguous correlation, keep
  protocol errors separate from method results, and avoid second graph/semantic
  rules. No typed-null fallback for missing/mistyped required fields.
- Events are separate bounded data/invalidation inputs, not request completion or
  automatic collection. Overflow/lost continuity is explicit to the consumer for
  bounded resync. Unrequested targets/sessions are not silently attached or read.
- Cancellation/timeout/detach stop new dispatch and invalidate affected tickets;
  late buffered stream data retains old correlation and never revives a ticket.
  Preserve already completed results according to their owner. Real mutation,
  fresh refs and host possible-effect permits remain H01/A01/W02 responsibilities;
  this library must not advertise peer receipt as their success or authorization.
- Keep error/Debug/log metadata bounded and sanitized. Untrusted peer error text
  and raw results cannot leak into diagnostics; raw data delivery is explicit.
  Preserve the accepted upstream log boundary at composition, no logger takeover.
