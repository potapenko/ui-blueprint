## FRONTIER.SCOPE

Opt-in `selection="geometry_frontier"` supplies positive `frontier_depth` within
the request's existing source-depth bound. Existing geometry_records behavior stays
unchanged. First establish the selected native window, source owner, exact container
path and all qualifying native witnesses under their existing contracts. No new
component discovery, text predicate, coordinate filter or first-match policy.

## FRONTIER.COLLECT

Count geometry-bearing source view records along each branch, starting at the
selected root. Presence of position or size metadata counts even when its cached
value later proves dirty, stale, incomplete or unknown. Do not decide depth from
known values or inspect models to choose a branch. Transparent/non-geometric
records retain their existing structural traversal and raw/depth/cycle charges.

At the declared geometric depth, retain that boundary record and stop descendants
except for the already qualified native witness path union. Every sibling at a
selected level is considered; neither a successful first branch nor missing data
permits dropping others. Witness context may extend below the frontier, retains
its actual depth and must be attributed separately. Unknown geometry stays unknown.
Do not fill omitted children with AX or ancestor rectangles. Native witness proof,
partial source ownership and root/native-window transforms keep their original meaning.

All actually visited structural records share the existing1024 raw budget and
original byte/call/node/deadline bounds. The frontier is a projection boundary,
not a larger acquisition allowance. Cycles, incomplete native binding and budget
failure refuse without a successful partial document.

## FRONTIER.OUTPUT

Keep canonical source geometry, identities, observations and transforms unchanged.
Report requested frontier depth, retained boundary node indices and deeper native
witness context separately in the capture manifest. Mark excluded descendants
and partial coverage explicitly; unknown branch order remains unknown. A geometry
skeleton never claims hidden framework details, complete source scope, visible
pixels, hit regions or atomic presented frames.

## FRONTIER.ACCEPT

Focused checks cover transparent records, missing/dirty geometry metadata, multiple
siblings, deep witness context, invalid depth, repeated-node cycle, raw-cap failure
and original identity/data preservation in integration. Qualify the composed required
Practice outer frames on the fixture, then unchanged donor in collapsed and changed
state. Compare raw cached bounds and public resolved anchors as separately sourced
facts; do not introduce rounding or a tolerance to hide their discrepancy.
Shared parser/collector/codec glue remains S13-owned until explicit handoff.
